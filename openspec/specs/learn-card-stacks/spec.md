# learn-card-stacks Specification

## Purpose

LEARN keeps two stacks of cards: auto (generated by sync, the sources and rebuild) and manual (cards the human asked
for). Manual cards are served first, the newest request on top, survive a rebuild, and guide how auto cards are
asked.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Every row and card names its stack
A ledger row and each of its cards SHALL carry `stack`, `auto` or `manual` (a row with no `stack` is `auto`).
`fleet learn add --manual` SHALL make a manual row with `stackAt` set to now; `fleet learn stack <lids> --manual`
SHALL move live rows to manual (a new row version, `stackAt` = now) and `--auto` back (no `stackAt`). Existing
cards SHALL follow in place, cid and schedule kept. Moving rows is LEARN's or the human's; any other role exits 4.

#### Scenario: Migrating rows already in the ledger
- **WHEN** `fleet learn stack L2-L3 --manual` runs on two auto rows with cards
- **THEN** both rows and all their cards say `stack: manual` and the cards keep their cids

#### Scenario: Control: a missing row
- **WHEN** `fleet learn stack L99 --manual` names a row that does not exist
- **THEN** it exits 1 and changes nothing

### Requirement: Manual cards are served first, the newest request on top
Lessons and due cards SHALL be ordered: manual rows grouped by topic, the topic with the newest `stackAt` first, rows
in lid order; then auto rows sharing a topic or work subject with a manual row; then the other auto rows. A topic
with rows on both stacks SHALL be listed as two lessons, the manual one on its own.

#### Scenario: A manual card ahead of earlier-due auto cards
- **WHEN** two auto cards came due a day before one manual card
- **THEN** `fleet learn due` and the quiz list the manual card first

### Requirement: Rebuild keeps the manual stack
`fleet learn rebuild` SHALL keep `learn/manual.jsonl`, re-mark as manual the rows the sources bring back, re-add the
hand-made manual rows with their original `stackAt`, and SHALL NOT bring back a manual row that was dropped.

#### Scenario: Hand-made manual card after a rebuild
- **WHEN** a manual card added with `fleet learn add --manual` has no source to regenerate it and LEARN rebuilds
- **THEN** the row is back, manual, with cards, and still served first

### Requirement: Manual cards guide auto cards
`fleet learn examples` SHALL list the manual stack (prompt and quote) newest request first. When learn.json
`autoPrompt.run` is set and the manual stack is not empty, a new untitled auto row's card prompt SHALL be the first
line the command prints that ends in `?`, is 12 to 160 characters and names neither the source ref nor the lane;
the command SHALL be killed at `timeoutSec` and any other outcome SHALL leave the template prompt.

#### Scenario: Control: autoPrompt is off with no manual cards
- **WHEN** autoPrompt is configured but no row is manual
- **THEN** the command is not run and the template prompt is used
